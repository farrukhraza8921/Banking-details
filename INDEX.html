Bank didn't fix it? Escalate.

Every bank must acknowledge a complaint and respond within its own timeline — usually 5 to 15 working days. If you're still unsatisfied, Pakistani law gives you two free, independent escalation routes before you'd ever need a lawyer.

  • Lodge the complaint directly with your bank first (in writing, or via app/branch) — this step is required before either route below will accept your case.
  • Still unresolved? File with the State Bank of Pakistan's Sunwai portal for general banking and digital-account issues.
  • For disputes the bank refuses to resolve fairly, approach the Banking Mohtasib Pakistan — an independent ombudsman that mediates free of charge.
